# Governance: Hitchwiki Maps

## 1. Preamble & Core Vision
This project directly resonates with and builds upon the core values established in the [Hitchwiki:How we want to keep Hitchwiki](https://hitchwiki.org/en/Hitchwiki:How_we_want_to_keep_Hitchwiki) vision. It is a living document meant to evolve, grow, and be appended based on community consensus.

### Our Motto
* **A Place for Learning:** Growth and skill-sharing are core to our existence.
* **Move Fast and Break Things:** Innovation requires experimentation; early failure is acceptable.
* **Fun & Camaraderie:** The journey of building should be as enjoyable as the adventure of hitchhiking.
* **Self-Efficacy:** Every contributor should clearly see the meaningful impact of their work on the global hitchhiking community and feel genuinely appreciated.

---

## 2. Decision-Making & Do-ocracy
As generative AI lowers engineering constraints, our primary bottlenecks shift from technical execution and developer time to capital (for AI capabilities and server infrastructure) and collective direction. We must remain agile, unburdened by administrative gridlock.

* **Do-ocracy:** Those who invest their time and energy earn the right to shape the project and its direction.
* **The Consensus Rule (Anti-Stagnation):** To implement any structural or behavioral change, at least **two (2) people must be in favor**, and **$\ge$ 50% of all actively cast voices** must approve.
* **The Velocity Clause:** No reaction or silence within a designated review window implies consent. A lack of response must never lead to a standstill; progress and change are always prioritized over stagnation.

---

## 3. Strategic Principles
Our ultimate goal is to promote hitchhiking as a sustainable, community-driven mode of transport, lower the barrier to entry, and transform it into a data-informed practice.

* **Human-Centric Real-World Data:** In an era of AI-generated noise ("slop"), authentic, human-experienced data and insights are incredibly valuable. We gather deep data to understand how hitchhiking and the society around it evolve.
* **Radical Openness:** All code is open-source, and all collected data is completely open and independent of our specific application. We build a shared, joint data store for rides so that anyone can construct alternative interfaces or use the data for academic research.
* **Mobile-First Design:** The tools must work flawlessly on the road, where hitchhikers need them most.
* **Deep Ecosystem Integration:** We actively link back and forth with Hitchwiki, embed OpenStreetMap (OSM) data, and map official carpooling or hitchhiking spots to provide actionable feedback to operators and cities.
* **Direct Value to the Hitchhiker:** Data must immediately serve the user on the road—whether through intelligent routing, real-time safety insights, or dynamic heatmaps to help them make faster, safer decisions.

---

## 4. Execution, Speed & Ownership
We actively minimize the cycle time from identifying an issue to its production deployment.

* **Responsive Code Review:** Maintainers and contributors aim to review any open Pull Request (PR) within 24 hours, or explicitly signal a clear timeline (e.g., "Review in $X$ days") to eliminate uncertainty.
* **Empowered Ownership:** The individual implementing a feature carries full responsibility for its lifecycle. This includes user validation/interviews (if needed), technical deployment, and short-term stability (triaging related bugs).
* **UI Iteration:** User interface design should not stall in endless theoretical debates. Bring it out into production, gather real-world usage data, and iterate rapidly.
* **Volunteering Context:** We honor that this is a volunteer project. Contributors have shifting priorities, travels, and life events. We support this by practicing radical transparency regarding our availability and schedules.

---

## 5. Technical & Paradigm Shifts
* **Agility Over Rigid Maintainability:** Because the cost of generating, refactoring, and rewriting code is approaching zero due to advanced AI capabilities, we intentionally sacrifice traditional, rigid maintainability in favor of deployment speed and feature experimentation.
* **Documentation Focus:** If technical debt is introduced for speed, we strongly document the *why* and the *how* of the architecture. The cost of a future cleanup or complete rewrite is minor; our time is better spent building the community and accelerating usage.
* **Scientific & Rational Discourse:** When opinions clash, we fall back on a scientific approach. We define success metrics, evaluate multiple options through data, and rely on collective rationality rather than subjective preference to settle the path forward.
